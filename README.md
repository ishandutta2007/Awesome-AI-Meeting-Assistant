## 📊 Market Overview & Industry Intelligence

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Market Size & Structure**: The global **AI Meeting Assistant and Speech Intelligence market** is estimated at **$2.5 Billion in 2026** (growing at a ~24% CAGR toward $5.8B by 2030). The sector is **moderately fragmented**: while category leaders (Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom) capture significant enterprise and SMB mindshare, no single vendor enjoys a "winner-take-all" monopoly. Specialized sales-coaching platforms (Avoma, Gong) and local privacy-first open-source tools flourish alongside desktop notetakers.

## 💻 Open-Source & Local-First GitHub Projects

Privacy-first and developer-friendly alternatives built on OpenAI Whisper, PyAnnote, and local LLMs (Ollama, Llama.cpp) for complete data sovereignty without recurring SaaS fees.

- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openai/whisper?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openai/whisper/stargazers) **[OpenAI Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper)** — General-purpose speech recognition model trained on 680,000 hours of multilingual data. The foundation of modern open-source meeting transcription.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ggerganov/whisper.cpp?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ggerganov/whisper.cpp/stargazers) **[whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggerganov/whisper.cpp)** — High-performance C/C++ port of OpenAI's Whisper model with zero dependencies, lightweight CPU/GPU execution for local meeting processing.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/pyannote/pyannote-audio?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio/stargazers) **[pyannote-audio](https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio)** — Neural toolkit written in Python for speaker diarization, speaker verification, and overlap detection in meeting recordings.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper/stargazers) **[faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper)** — Re-implementation of OpenAI Whisper using CTranslate2, delivering up to 4x faster transcription speed with lower memory usage.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily/stargazers) **[Meetily](https://github.com/Zackriya-Solutions/meetily)** — Popular open-source, self-hosted AI meeting notetaker (MIT). Captures system audio with local Whisper transcription and Ollama LLM summaries without sending data to third parties.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/estebanstifli/Meet2Notes?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/estebanstifli/Meet2Notes/stargazers) **[Meet2Notes](https://github.com/estebanstifli/Meet2Notes)** — Local-first, self-hosted AI meeting assistant for transcription, speaker diarization, and structured notes on Windows, macOS, and Linux.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/elmoghany/meeting-scribe?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/elmoghany/meeting-scribe/stargazers) **[MeetingScribe](https://github.com/elmoghany/meeting-scribe)** — Local, key-free MIT meeting-notes agent for Google Meet and Zoom. Features live transcription, diarization, AI summaries, and "ask your meeting" chat without cloud APIs.
- [![Star](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/TARIFUDDIN/Memoir-Ai?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/TARIFUDDIN/Memoir-Ai/stargazers) **[Memoir-Ai](https://github.com/TARIFUDDIN/Memoir-Ai)** — Open-source enterprise-style meeting intelligence platform aiming at autonomous recording, diarization, global RAG across meetings, and Jira/Slack integrations.
